Support

Write to support@equalinformation.com. Include your device model, the app version (Settings, bottom of the page) and what you were doing. We read every message.

Report a content error

Tell us the app, the screen and the statement, and ideally the source you checked. Every lesson names its sources, and we correct errors we are told about.

Subscriptions

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
  • Restore: open MedForge, Settings, Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Text too small or too large?

Use the A− and A+ buttons at the top of every screen, from 40% to 300%.
