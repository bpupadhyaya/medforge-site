Spirometry Lab

Read the loop. Name the lung disease.

Eight patients, each with a live flow-volume loop and test results. Decide the pattern, the bronchodilator response and the gas transfer, then name the diagnosis.

Free preview: the first three cases. Unlock for all eight.   Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Try it: read the loop

Pick a patient. The dashed loop is the predicted normal. Work the four steps.

What you will learn

  • Obstructive, restrictive and fixed upper airway patterns, and what each loop looks like
  • Bronchodilator reversibility and the meaning of a low or normal DLCO
  • How to separate asthma, COPD, interstitial lung disease, obesity and neuromuscular weakness
