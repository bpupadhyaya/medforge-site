Where the facts come from

MedForge draws on the world's most authoritative medical and public-health institutions. Today's lessons cite the U.S. National Library of Medicine; the list below shows the families of sources we are drawing from as the library grows.

  • NIH / NLM: National Institutes of Health and the National Library of Medicine (StatPearls on NCBI Bookshelf is used today)
  • WHO: World Health Organization
  • CDC: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
  • FDA: U.S. Food and Drug Administration
  • UKRI / MRC: UK Research and Innovation, Medical Research Council
  • CIHR: Canadian Institutes of Health Research
  • NHMRC: National Health and Medical Research Council (Australia)
  • AMED / RIKEN IMS: Japan's medical research agency and RIKEN Center for Integrative Medical Sciences
  • ICMR: Indian Council of Medical Research
  • CAMS / PUMC: Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences and Peking Union Medical College
  • RKI: Robert Koch Institute
  • Inserm: French National Institute of Health and Medical Research
  • NAM, AHRQ, HRSA, CMS: National Academy of Medicine and other U.S. health agencies
  • EMA, NICE: European Medicines Agency and UK National Institute for Health and Care Excellence
