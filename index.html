Who it is for

One library, three ways to use it

MedForge is built for people who learn medicine and for people who practice it, in any health system.

Medical students

Learn it so it stays

Work through the organ systems and the reasoning skills a rigorous MD curriculum expects: the ECG, the blood gas, the lesion, the nephron. Each answer explains the mechanism, so you can rebuild a fact instead of memorising it.

Residents and practicing doctors

Refresh what you do not see every day

Short, focused drills for the rhythm, the dose, the differential or the algorithm you want sharp before a shift or a rotation. Every statement shows the guideline or reference behind it, so you can check it in seconds.

Emergency and surgical teams

Rehearse the first ten minutes

Branching trauma and chest-pain cases built around the decisions that cannot wait, with the reasoning for each step and the traps. Designed for the pace and standards of a busy resuscitation bay anywhere in the world.

How a lesson works

Play it. Understand it. Check it.

Play

Commit to an answer: a rhythm, a lesion site, the next step in the primary survey. Mistakes cost points, not patients.

Understand

Every answer explains the mechanism, so side effects, findings and first actions follow from first principles instead of memorized lists.

Check the source

Each explanation carries numbered citations. Tap one and the original page opens in a reading window, with back and forward, right where you left off.

How we cite

No source, no claim.

Every lesson in every app is written from named, authoritative public sources and carries its citations on the same screen as the fact.

  • Facts are paraphrased from the cited source, never copied.
  • Every link is checked automatically to confirm it is live, and each source records the date we accessed it.
  • We lead with the U.S. National Library of Medicine, NHLBI and FDA labels, add WHO and NICE where they apply, and bring in more national agencies and specialty guidelines as the library grows.
  • Sources are listed, in full, on the Sources page.

Questions

Before you subscribe

Is MedForge medical advice?

No. It is an educational product. It must not be used to diagnose or treat patients, and it does not replace your institution's protocols, current guidelines or clinical judgement.

Where do the facts come from?

From named public authorities such as the U.S. National Library of Medicine (StatPearls, MedlinePlus), NHLBI, FDA labels on DailyMed, WHO and NICE. Every explanation shows its citations, and each opens the original page inside the app.

Does it cover guidelines outside the United States?

Some lessons already cite UK and WHO sources. A dedicated shelf for regional guideline differences (Europe, UK, Japan, India, China, Brazil, South Africa and others) is on the roadmap and marked as planned.

How is it different from a question bank or a textbook?

You act instead of read: you read the strip, order the clue, choose the next step. Then the explanation shows the mechanism and its sources. It is meant to sit beside your textbooks and question banks, not replace them.

Can I buy a single app?

Yes. Every app is its own subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free preview first. You only pay for the subjects you use.

Do you collect my data?

No accounts, no advertising, no trackers. Your progress stays on your device. See the privacy page.

What if I find an error?

Medicine changes and we can make mistakes. Each lesson names its sources, so you can check it, and we correct errors we are told about. Write to support@equalinformation.com.
